# Economics

## Assumptions

This tool is a B2B lead-generation product for Credex. The primary business value comes from finding startups that are already spending meaningfully on AI tools or API credits.

Rough assumptions:

- Average qualified startup AI spend: $2,000/month
- Average savings opportunity: 20%
- Estimated monthly savings surfaced: $400/month
- Annual savings surfaced: $4,800/year
- Credex earns value by helping companies access discounted AI credits or optimization support

## Lead Value Estimate

If a high-intent lead is spending $2,000/month on AI infrastructure and Credex can help redirect or optimize even part of that spend, the lead could be worth several hundred to a few thousand dollars annually.
