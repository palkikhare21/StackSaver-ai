    # Metrics

## North Star Metric

The North Star metric is **completed audits with captured email**.

This is better than daily active users because StackSaver AI is not a daily-use product. A founder or engineering manager may only need this tool when reviewing spend, planning budget, or investigating a high AI bill.

A completed audit with captured email means the user received value first and then trusted the product enough to save the report.

## Input Metrics

### 1. Audit Start Rate

Measures how many landing page visitors click “Start audit.”

This shows whether the landing page clearly communicates the problem and value.

### 2. Audit Completion Rate

Measures how many users who start the form actually submit it and see results.

This shows whether the form is simple enough and not too long or confusing.

### 3. Lead Capture Rate

Measures how many users enter their email after seeing the audit result.

This shows whether the savings result feels valuable enough to save or discuss.
