## FAQ

### Does this connect to my billing account?

No. This version is a manual audit tool. You enter your AI tools and monthly spend, and the app estimates savings opportunities.

### Who is this for?

Startup founders, engineering managers, and lean teams using multiple AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini, and APIs.

### How accurate are the savings estimates?

The estimates are based on pricing rules and usage-fit heuristics. Enterprise contracts and custom pricing may vary.

### Do I need to log in?

No. Users can run the audit without creating an account. Email capture happens only after the audit result is shown.

### Why does Credex matter here?

For high-savings cases, Credex may help teams capture more savings through discounted AI infrastructure credits and spend optimization guidance.
