# Go-To-Market Plan

## Target User

The exact target user is a founder, CTO, or engineering manager at a seed to Series A startup with 5–50 employees. They are already using multiple AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, and OpenAI or Anthropic APIs. They do not have a dedicated finance operations person yet, so software spend reviews happen manually and usually only when cash becomes tight.

## Trigger Moment

They would want this tool right after seeing a surprisingly high monthly AI bill, adding new developer seats, or realizing that several employees are using overlapping AI tools. They may be searching or scrolling through topics like:

- “reduce AI API cost”
- “Cursor vs Copilot pricing”
- “ChatGPT Team worth it”
- “OpenAI API bill too high”
- “startup software spend optimization”

## Where They Hang Out

Specific channels I would target:

- X/Twitter posts from indie hackers and startup founders discussing burn rate
- Indie Hackers community
- r/SaaS
- r/startups
- r/LocalLLaMA for AI infrastructure cost discussions
- Hacker News launch posts and comment threads
- Founder-focused Slack or Discord communities
- LinkedIn posts from founders talking about reducing burn

## First 100 Users in 30 Days With $0 Budget

I would start by personally reaching out to founders and engineering leads with a short message:

> I built a free AI spend audit tool that estimates whether your team is overpaying for tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Copilot, and APIs. It takes less than 3 minutes and shows monthly + annual savings. Would you be open to trying it?

Execution plan:

Week 1: DM 40 founders and engineering managers from LinkedIn, X, and college/startup networks. Ask for feedback, not sales.

Week 2: Post a short teardown thread on X: “I audited a fake 12-person startup’s AI bill and found $742/month in savings.” Link the tool.

Week 3: Share the tool in r/SaaS and Indie Hackers with a transparent build-in-public post.

Week 4: Publish one blog-style post: “How startups quietly overspend on AI tools.” Include screenshots and a free audit CTA.

## Unfair Distribution Channel

Credex’s unfair channel is its access to real discounted AI infrastructure credits. Most generic cost tools can only say “reduce usage.” Credex can actually help users capture savings through discounted credits when the audit finds high API or enterprise spend.

## Week-1 Traction Goal

If this works, week-1 traction should look like:

- 50 unique visitors
- 25 completed audits
- 10 saved reports
- 3 high-savings leads over $500/month
- 1–2 consultation requests

The main signal is not daily active users. This is a utility tool, so the important signal is completed audits and qualified leads.
